# nRF7002 DK adapter

Holds a Nordic [nRF7002 DK](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Development-hardware/nRF7002-DK) upright on the OWL, antenna end up, with its centre of mass on the rotation axis.

Fix the DK with four M2.5 × 10 screws, from the front through the board into M2.5 hex nuts pressed into the back of the bosses. Nylon screws and nuts are recommended.

The interface MCU's micro-USB port (J2) on the bottom edge sits right above the adapter, 24 mm up. Use an angled micro-USB cable.
