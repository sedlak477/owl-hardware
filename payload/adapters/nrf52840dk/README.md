# nRF52840 DK adapter

Holds a Nordic [nRF52840 DK](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Development-hardware/nRF52840-DK) upright on the OWL.

Fix the DK to the plate with M2.5 screws. Nylon screws are recommended.

The DK faces 12° further clockwise (seen from above) than on the old v1.2.2 adapter, so that its plate clears the alignment grooves.
